# StudioZYRN site

The studio's web pages, including the privacy policy Google Play asks for.
Plain HTML, no build step. Published with GitHub Pages from its own public
repo, not from this one: copy this folder's contents to the root of
`studiozyrn.github.io`.

Pages:

- `index.html`: the studio's page.
- `for-the-love-of-flags/privacy/`: the game's privacy policy. Its address,
  once published, is `https://studiozyrn.github.io/for-the-love-of-flags/privacy/`.
  That address goes in the Play Console and in the game's Settings.

## Publishing

1. On GitHub, create a free organisation named `StudioZYRN`.
2. In it, create a public repo named `studiozyrn.github.io`.
3. Push this folder's contents to its `main` branch. Pages turns on by
   itself for a repo with that name; the site is live a minute or two later.

## Keep it true

The policy describes the game as it ships: AdMob ads, a Google Play
purchase, nothing sent to StudioZYRN. Update it, and its date, before any of
these change: a new ad network or analytics, anything sent to a server,
accounts, or a new permission. It must also match the Data safety form in
the Play Console.
