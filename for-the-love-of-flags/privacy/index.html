Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 Oct 2026

For the Love of Flags is a flag guessing game made by StudioZYRN. This page explains what information the game uses, where it is kept, and who else sees any of it. If you have a question, email zariyanmansoor@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and StudioZYRN runs no servers. What you put into the game stays on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes everything it kept.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps these in its own private storage on your device. They are never sent to StudioZYRN or anyone else:

  • Your player card: the name you type (any name you like; it need not be your real one) and the flag you pick.
  • An age band: the game asks the year you were born only to choose the right ad settings (see "Younger players"). It keeps a band, such as "under 13", and not the year itself.
  • Your play: levels cleared, streaks, flags answered, a round left unfinished, and the day you started.

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the game's data in your own Google backup, so it can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is governed by your settings and Google's policies. StudioZYRN cannot see it.

Motion sensors

Some parts of the game lean as you tilt your phone. The game reads the phone's motion sensors while it is open to do this. The readings are used on the spot and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Saving and sharing your card

When you tap Save, the game saves a picture of your player card to your photos; to do this it asks for permission to add photos. When you tap Share, the picture goes to the app you choose, under that app's own terms. Nothing is saved or shared unless you tap.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, including ads you can choose to watch for an extra life. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • information about your device and the app, such as its model, operating system and language,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out,
  • how you interact with ads.

This information goes to Google, not to StudioZYRN. How Google uses it is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law asks for it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks your consent before ads can be personalised, using Google's consent form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

You can pay once to remove ads. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. StudioZYRN never receives your card or payment details; Google Play only tells the game that the purchase was made. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Younger players

The game is made for everyone, including children. The birth year it asks for is used only to choose ad settings: for younger players, ads are not personalised and are requested under Google's settings for children. StudioZYRN does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and never from children. If you are a parent and have a question, email us.

Your choices

  • You can change your name and flag in the game at any time.
  • To delete everything the game keeps, uninstall it, or clear its data in your phone's settings. Because StudioZYRN holds no copy, there is nothing further to delete on our side.
  • For information Google holds through ads or Google Play, use your Google account's controls at myaccount.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, this page will be updated before it does, and the date at the top will change.
